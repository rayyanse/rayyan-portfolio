SEO-Ray privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026 · Last updated 16 November 2026

This policy explains what data SEO-Ray accesses, why, where it's kept, who sees it and how to have it deleted. It covers SEO-Ray, the website audit and setup tool described at rayyankhan.dev/seo-ray.

Who we are

SEO-Ray is built and operated by Agha Muhammad Rayyan Khan, trading as Rawaiz Tech, in Lahore, Pakistan ("we", "us"). We're responsible for the data described here. Contact: seo@rayyankhan.dev.

We use SEO-Ray to audit and set up websites for site owners who ask us to. SEO-Ray is not a public self-sign-up service.

Google user data we access

Only if a site owner chooses to connect their Google account, by signing in on Google's consent screen. SEO-Ray asks for these permissions and accesses this data:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/webmasters: the list of Search Console properties the account can see; for the websites you ask us to work on, search performance data (search queries, pages, clicks, impressions, click-through rate, average position), URL Inspection results (index status, Google's chosen canonical, last crawl time) and sitemap status. We also use it to add your site to Search Console and submit its sitemap.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/siteverification: a verification code for the domain you asked us to set up, the list of domains the account has verified, and the request asking Google to verify that domain.
  • openid and email: your Google account's email address.

We don't access Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts or any other Google data, and we don't request any other permissions.

How we use Google user data

  • To produce the audit reports for your website (a plain-English report for the owner and a technical one for their developer).
  • To verify your domain, add it to Search Console and submit its sitemap, when you ask us to set the site up.
  • To show progress over time for your site (for example, before and after a fix).
  • To label the connection with your email, so we know which account belongs to which site.

We don't use Google user data for advertising, we don't sell it, we don't use it to decide credit or lending, and we don't use it to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models, generalized or otherwise. No one reads it except to provide the work you asked for, to fix a security problem, or where the law requires.

Limited Use. SEO-Ray's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

Google user data about your website appears only in the reports we send to you, the site owner, and to the developer you name for the site. We don't share it with anyone else, and we don't transfer it to third parties except where the law requires it or as part of a merger or sale of the business, in which case this policy would still apply.

Separately from Google user data: to check whether AI assistants know a business, SEO-Ray sends the business's public homepage title and description to Google's Gemini API. That's public website text, not data from your Google account.

Storage and security

  • SEO-Ray currently runs on our own work computer. Audit results, including Search Console data for your site, are kept in a local database on that computer.
  • For a connected Google account we keep only the refresh token, in a credentials file that is excluded from our code repository. Access tokens last one hour and are never written to disk.
  • All traffic to and from Google uses HTTPS. The computer is password-protected and only Agha Muhammad Rayyan Khan has access to it. Secrets aren't written to logs.

If we move SEO-Ray to hosted servers, we'll update this policy before that happens.

Keeping and deleting data

  • We keep your site's audit history while we're working on your site, so we can show before-and-after results.
  • When the work ends, or whenever you ask, we delete the stored refresh token straight away and your site's data within 30 days.
  • You can revoke SEO-Ray's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking stops all further access; email us if you also want the stored data deleted.

Other data

  • Public website content. SEO-Ray crawls the public pages of the sites you ask us to audit, the same way a search engine does, and measures their speed with Google PageSpeed Insights.
  • Bing Webmaster Tools. If you give us access, SEO-Ray adds and verifies your site in Bing, submits its sitemap and reads its backlink counts.
  • Email. If you email us, we keep the conversation to do the work and answer you.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the data we hold about you or your site, or stop working on your site, by emailing seo@rayyankhan.dev. We answer within 7 days. SEO-Ray isn't meant for children and we don't knowingly collect data about anyone under 16.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and tell site owners with a connected account before the change applies to them.
