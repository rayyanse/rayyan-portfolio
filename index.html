    How I work

    Simple process. Clear handoff. No black box.

    The goal isn't just a system that works. It's a system you understand well enough to explain to your own team without calling me.

    1. 01

      Audit

      I map your current process and find where automation saves the most hours, not the most impressive demo.

    2. 02

      Blueprint

      I design the workflow, tools, data flow, edge cases and failure handling, before writing anything.

    3. 03

      Build

      I build and test against real scenarios and messy real data, not the happy path that always works in a demo.

    4. 04

      Handoff

      You get documentation, workflow access and a recorded walkthrough. The system is yours, not rented from me.
