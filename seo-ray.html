See your website the way Google and AI assistants do.

SEO-Ray crawls a site, measures its speed on a phone, checks what Google has indexed and whether ChatGPT-style assistants can read it, then writes two reports: one in plain English for the owner, one with the exact fixes for the developer. With the owner's permission it also sets the site up in Google Search Console and Bing.

What it checks

Every rule is checked against Google's own documentation before it goes in.

  • Technical healthRobots rules, redirects, canonical tags, sitemaps, HTTPS, broken pages.
  • Speed on a phoneGoogle's real-visitor data where it exists, Lighthouse lab runs where it doesn't.
  • What Google indexedWhich pages Google has, and which it skipped and why.
  • AI assistantsWhether AI search bots are allowed in, and whether Gemini knows the business.
  • Your name on GoogleWhat people see when they search the owner's or the business's name.
  • Structured dataSchema markup validated against schema.org and Google's required fields.

What SEO-Ray does with your Google account

Only if you choose to connect it. You sign in on Google's own screen and can disconnect at any time.

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/webmasters

Search Console: view and manage your verified sites

Used to add your website to Search Console, submit its sitemap, and read its search performance (searches, clicks, impressions, positions) and Google's index status for its key pages. That data goes into the reports for your site and nowhere else. The read-only permission can't add a site or submit a sitemap, which is why this one is needed.

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/siteverification

Site verification: manage the sites you control

Used to get a verification code for the domain you asked us to set up and to ask Google to confirm it, after SEO-Ray publishes the code as a DNS record. Nothing else is verified or changed.

openid · email

Your Google account email

Used to label the connection, so we know which account belongs to which website.

What SEO-Ray never does

It doesn't read Gmail, Drive, Calendar or any other Google data, doesn't sell or share your data, doesn't use it for advertising, and doesn't use it to train AI models. It only works on the websites you ask us to work on.

SEO-Ray's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Details are in the privacy policy.
