Questions

What clients ask first

Official WhatsApp API or my normal number?

Both are possible. The WhatsApp Lead System and Al Munir use Meta's official Cloud API. Bresco links each agent's own WhatsApp number to one dashboard. I'll tell you which fits your volume and team, and the trade-offs of each.

Can the bot reply in Roman Urdu?

Yes. The bots on Bresco and Al Munir reply in Roman Urdu and English, the way customers actually write.

What happens when a customer wants a real person?

A person takes over in the same WhatsApp chat. On Al Munir's pipeline that happens the moment a customer asks for one.

Can it send quotes or files?

Yes. Al Munir's pipeline prices the order and sends a two-page PDF quote from the company's own branded template.

Who owns the system when it's done?

You do. You get documentation, workflow access and a recorded walkthrough. The system is yours, not rented from me.
