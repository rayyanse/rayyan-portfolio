Questions

What clients ask first.

Can the agent speak Urdu?

Yes. Call Stream's agents talk to callers in Urdu, and the same setups work in English. On WhatsApp, the bots handle Roman Urdu too.

Do I need a new phone number or a SIP trunk?

No. Call Stream runs on a normal Pakistani SIM in an Android phone, so there's no PTA paperwork or carrier deal. The limit is one call at a time per SIM, which suits a business doing around 20 calls a day. For more volume, we plan a proper phone line together.

Can it book appointments into my calendar?

Yes. The booking agent checks real Google Calendar availability, confirms a slot with the caller, books it and sends a confirmation email automatically.

What does it do when it can't help a caller?

You decide that in the blueprint: hand over to a person, take a message, or follow up on WhatsApp. Nothing is left to a guess.

Who owns the system when it's done?

You do. You get documentation, workflow access and a recorded walkthrough. The system is yours, not rented from me.
