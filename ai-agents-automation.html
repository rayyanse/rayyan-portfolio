How I work

Simple process. Clear handoff.

The goal isn't just a system that works. It's one you understand well enough to explain to your own team without calling me.

  1. 01

    Audit

    I map your current process and find where automation saves the most hours, not the most impressive demo.

  2. 02

  3. 03

Questions

What clients ask first.

What's the difference between a chatbot and an AI agent?

A chatbot answers questions. An agent decides what needs to happen and does it: updates the CRM, books the visit, sends the quote. That's the part I build.

Which tools do you build with?

n8n, Make.com and Zapier for workflows; Claude, OpenAI, Gemini and Groq for the AI; Next.js and Supabase when the system needs its own app.

What happens if the AI provider goes down?

Critical systems get a fallback chain. Jennifer falls back through a four-step provider chain, so one provider having a bad day doesn't stop the work.

How do we start?

Send me the workflow, your tool stack, or just describe the manual thing that eats your week. I'll tell you what can be automated, what shouldn't be, and what the first useful version looks like.

Who owns the system when it's done?

You do. You get documentation, workflow access and a recorded walkthrough. The system is yours, not rented from me.
