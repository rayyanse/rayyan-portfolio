An Urdu AI agent on the number you already have.

Call Stream turns an Android phone and a normal Pakistani SIM into an AI call agent that answers in Urdu. No SIP trunk, no PTA paperwork, no new number on your shop board. A property dealer runs it on his own line today. Built together with Shahwaiz.

The problem

Every AI voice vendor in Pakistan opens with the same sentence: “first, get a SIP trunk.”

Case 001 · Call Stream

That means PTA paperwork, weeks of procurement and a brand new number nobody has ever called. For a business whose only line is printed on its shop board, that's a dead end.

How it works

Step by step, on real data.

Example call for illustration · AI voices

01 / 04
  1. 01

    The customer calls your normal number

    The call lands on a live mobile SIM in an Android phone, exactly like it does today.

  2. 02

    The phone hands the call to a bridge

    Over Bluetooth into an Asterisk bridge, then out to the AI agent over SIP, encrypted with TLS.

  3. 03

    The agent knows who's calling

    The caller's real number travels with the call, so the agent knows who it is before it says a word.

  4. 04

    It talks in Urdu, both ways

    Callers are answered in Urdu, and the same setup phones new leads back. Bresco Properties uses it to call every lead.

Under the hood

The decisions that mattered

  • 01

    The phone number was the hard part

    Most AI voice talk here is about which model sounds better in Urdu. The real blocker was getting a call off a normal SIM and into an agent without a carrier deal.

  • 02

    TLS on port 5061

    The week that nearly killed it went to the ISP swallowing SIP on port 5060: every INVITE out, nothing back, not even a 100 Trying. Moving to TLS on 5061 fixed it.

  • 03

    A demo film for every industry

    I made eight vertical demo films, one per industry: Umrah travel, real estate, healthcare, education, study abroad, BPO, AI agencies and car showrooms.

Honest limits

One SIM handles one call at a time. That's fine for a business doing 20 calls a day, not 200.
