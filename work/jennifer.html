An AI employee for the real estate lead desk.

Jennifer manages leads, scores intent, tracks follow-ups, searches live market data, keeps property inventory current and delivers a daily business briefing, all through a normal chat. Built end to end in Next.js and Supabase.

The problem

Real estate teams bleed leads when follow-ups, property matching and CRM updates all depend on someone remembering to do them.

Agents juggle WhatsApp chats, spreadsheets and site visits. The follow-up that gets forgotten is the deal that gets lost.

How it works

Step by step

  1. Tell it what happened, in plain words

    An agent says what happened with a lead. Jennifer decides the action from the conversation itself.

  2. It updates the CRM itself

    Lead status, intent score, tasks and follow-ups are written to the database. Nobody updates a CRM by hand.

  3. It answers from your own data

    RAG over the agency's knowledge plus live database context, and live market search when the question needs it.

  4. It briefs the owner every day

    A daily business briefing: new leads, follow-ups due and inventory changes.

Under the hood

The decisions that mattered

  • A four-step fallback chain

    Requests fall back through a four-step chain across Groq, OpenRouter and Gemini, so a single provider having a bad day doesn't stop the lead desk.

  • Permissions by role

    Supabase row-level security keeps each person to the data their role allows: Super Admin, Admin or Agent.

  • Built for agents on the move

    It installs on a phone as an app (PWA), because agents spend the day at site visits, not at a desk.
