An AI teammate inside real WhatsApp calls.

Sebastian lives inside ordinary WhatsApp and phone calls on your own Android phone. He introduces himself, stays quiet and takes notes. Say his name and he answers out loud, so both people hear him. He can search the web, book a follow-up or read a Notion doc mid-call, and the summary lands on WhatsApp when you hang up. Built for AssemblyAI's voice hackathon week.

The problem

Meeting bots like Otter and Fireflies only listen, and only inside meeting apps. A WhatsApp call has no seat for them.

Case 006 · Sebastian

And they never talk back. When someone asks a question mid-call, a note-taker writes it down. It can't look the answer up, book the follow-up or say a word.

How it works

Step by step, on real data.

Showcase film · the call section is a real WhatsApp call recording

01 / 06
  1. 01

    “Hey Sebastian, call Shahwaiz”

    An on-device wake word hears it, and Sebastian places the WhatsApp call. You can also just call someone yourself.

  2. 02

    He introduces himself, then listens

    He waits for the other side's first words before his join line, so a ringing tone never fools him and everyone knows they're being noted.

  3. 03

    Two transcripts, one per side

    Each side of the call gets its own live transcript, so every line knows who said it.

  4. 04

    Say his name and he answers out loud

    His voice is mixed into both sides of the call, so both people hear him. If someone talks over him, he stops.

  5. 05

    He does things mid-call

    Web search, booking a follow-up, reading a Notion doc. For a slow tool he says “On it.” first, then gives the result.

  6. 06

    The notes arrive when you hang up

    A summary with decisions and action items lands on WhatsApp, and the full notes go to a Notion page.

Under the hood

The decisions that mattered

  • 01

    Getting into WhatsApp's audio

    Android gives apps no way into another app's call audio. A helper running as Android's shell user registers two audio loopback mixes on WhatsApp: one takes the other person's voice off the earpiece, the other replaces what WhatsApp hears as the microphone.

  • 02

    Knowing when to speak

    A finished line with his name triggers him. For 10 seconds after he answers, follow-ups go to him too, he stays silent when people are talking to each other, and a passing mention of his name doesn't wake him.

  • 03

    He never hears himself

    While he speaks, both transcripts get silence, so his own voice can't trigger him or end up in the notes. The call-audio layer comes from Call Stream.

Honest limits

Built in a hackathon week. It needs an Android 13+ phone paired once over Wireless debugging, and it speaks English for now: Urdu waits on AssemblyAI's text-to-speech supporting it.
