The problem

When a team sells from WhatsApp, five agents means five numbers, five inboxes and a lead list someone splits by hand.

Bresco wanted every lead answered and called, without adding people to the lead desk.

How it works

Step by step. 5 moves.

The film on the left is the system running. The steps follow it from first contact to the last write.

Teaser cut · example data for illustration

01 / 05
  1. 01

    AI drafts the post, a person approves

    Facebook posts are written by AI and only go out after someone on the team approves them.

  2. 02

    Every agent's number sends it

    One team post goes out from every agent's own WhatsApp number.

  3. 03

    Leads split themselves

    Leads import from Excel and split automatically across the agents who are online.

  4. 04

    AI answers, people take over

    An AI replies in Roman Urdu and English, with a handoff to the agent, all in one shared inbox.

  5. 05

    Every lead gets a call in Urdu

    A calling agent phones each lead in Urdu. The calls run on Call Stream.

Under the hood

The decisions that mattered.

  • 01

    Agents keep their own numbers

    Each agent links their own WhatsApp number to the dashboard. There's no shared company number to set up.

  • 02

    One inbox for the whole team

    Every agent's chats land in one unified inbox, so anyone can see which lead has been answered.

  • 03

    Calls on Call Stream

    The Urdu calling runs on Call Stream, the SIM-based call agent built with Shahwaiz.
