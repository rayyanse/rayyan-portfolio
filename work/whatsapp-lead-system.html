The problem

Leads click the ad and land on WhatsApp. If the team is busy, the lead goes cold and the ad money is wasted.

Case 005 · WhatsApp Lead Management System

BGMC runs Instagram ads for consulting services. The fix had to answer every lead instantly and still put a real associate in the conversation.

How it works

Step by step, on real data.

Walkthrough film · names and numbers are sample data

01 / 05
  1. 01

    A lead clicks the ad

    They land on WhatsApp and the chatbot replies straight away.

  2. 02

    It collects the requirements

    A natural conversation, not a form, gathers what the lead needs.

  3. 03

    It picks the right person

    It detects which service the lead wants and assigns them round-robin to an associate who's available.

  4. 04

    The associate gets the summary

    An email lands with the full lead summary, and the associate continues the same WhatsApp chat.

  5. 05

    Everything is logged

    Every lead and follow-up is tracked in Google Sheets.

Under the hood

The decisions that mattered

  • 01

    Round-robin with availability

    Leads only go to associates who are available, so nobody gets a lead they can't answer.

  • 02

    Same chat, no app switching

    The associate picks up the conversation where the bot left it. The customer never changes apps.

  • 03

    Sheets as the log

    A simple shared sheet is the record, so the team didn't need a new CRM to start.
