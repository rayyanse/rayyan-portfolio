The problem

Leads click the ad and land on WhatsApp. If the team is busy, the lead goes cold and the ad money is wasted.

BGMC runs Instagram ads for consulting services. The fix had to answer every lead instantly and still put a real associate in the conversation.

How it works

Step by step. 5 moves.

The film on the left is the system running. The steps follow it from first contact to the last write.

Under the hood

The decisions that mattered.

  • 01

    Round-robin with availability

    Leads only go to associates who are available, so nobody gets a lead they can't answer.

  • 02

    Same chat, no app switching

    The associate picks up the conversation where the bot left it. The customer never changes apps.

  • 03

    Sheets as the log

    A simple shared sheet is the record, so the team didn't need a new CRM to start.
